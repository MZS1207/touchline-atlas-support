Privacy policy

Touchline Atlas Mobile · Updated 3 October 2026

Your game stays on your device

Career saves, settings, manager profiles, imported world databases and selected images are stored locally. The app does not operate user accounts, advertising, tracking or an analytics service, and does not send your career or images to the developer.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. The app reads verified purchase information to unlock the Editor and deliver investment funds. Local purchase records contain transaction identifiers, an anonymous purchase token, the destination career identifier and the investment amount. They help prevent duplicate or lost delivery; the app does not receive your payment card details.

Files and photos

The app accesses files and images that you choose to import, export or use in the game. Exported files are saved where you select. If you share them with someone or place them in a cloud-synced folder, that service's privacy policy applies.

Local diagnostics

The app keeps local diagnostics for interrupted sessions, save errors and career integrity problems. These are used to investigate problems on your device; the app does not automatically upload them. Apple may collect its own diagnostics depending on your system settings.

Retention and deletion

Local saves and settings remain until you remove them. You can delete careers in the game and remove imported images through their selection controls. Purchase delivery records are kept separately to prevent the same purchase from being delivered again. Removing all app data also removes these records and may prevent recovery of consumed investment packs. Deleting the iOS app removes its local data; device backups may retain earlier copies. Apple retains its purchase records under its own policies.

Sharing and changes

The app does not sell personal data or share it with advertising or analytics providers. If future versions add online services, this policy will be updated before those services are introduced.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact m.stevanovic1207@icloud.com. If you send us an email, we use the information you choose to provide to answer your request. Do not include payment details or send a career save unless it is needed to investigate a problem.
