Touchline Atlas support

Contact the developer

Email m.stevanovic1207@icloud.com with the app version, device model and steps that reproduce your issue. Do not include payment details or send a career save unless requested for troubleshooting.

Careers

Start a career from the welcome screen or continue a saved career. Saves stay locally on your device. Keep the app installed to retain its local files. Manage saved careers through the game’s career controls.

Purchases

Use Restore purchases in the Club Shop to restore the world editor or retry an unfinished purchase. Investment packs add virtual game funds to their original career and are consumed on delivery. They cannot be restored after all app data is removed. Funds have no real-world monetary value.
